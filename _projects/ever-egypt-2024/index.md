## Vehicle
Two 4 kW hub motors, two 24 V LiFePO4 batteries (48 V total, 80 Ah), motor controllers, BMS, DC-DC converter, lighting and safety switches.

## Tractive System Schematic
High-voltage schematics from the batteries through Anderson connectors, key switch, 200 A fuse and contactor to two 4 kW brushless motor controllers, shunt resistors and the DC-DC converter. Specified wire gauge (2 AWG for main power), lengths and color coding, with wiring tables for manufacturing.

## Low-Voltage System
A 12 V architecture powered by a DC-DC converter for running, indicator and brake lights, horn, interior light, hazard switch, ignition and data acquisition. Wires sized 12-18 AWG, 1 A and 2 A fuses, common ground and 12 V busbars.

## 3D Wiring & Installation
3D harness models in SolidWorks validated cable paths, clearances and fixation inside the chassis. On the vehicle, I installed the battery packs, controllers, DC-DC converter, shunt resistors, throttle pedal, kill switches and fuse box, with MF connectors, shrink wrap and secured harnesses.
