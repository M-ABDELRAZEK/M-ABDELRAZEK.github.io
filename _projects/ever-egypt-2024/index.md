## Accomplishments

### 1. Tractive System Wiring Schematic & Documentation
Designed complete high‑voltage wiring schematics for the powertrain, including connections from batteries to Anderson connectors, main key‑switch, power fuse (200 A), contactor, two brushless DC motor controllers (4 kW each), shunt resistors, and DC‑DC converter. Specified wire gauge (2 AWG for main power), lengths, and color coding. Created detailed wiring tables using SolidWorks – enabling precise manufacturing and assembly.

### 2. Low Voltage System Design & Integration
Developed 12 V low voltage architecture powered by a DC‑DC converter, comprising front/rear running lights, indicator lights, brake lights, horn, interior light, hazard switch, ignition, and data acquisition system. Sized wires (12–18 AWG), selected fuses (1 A, 2 A for lights), and distributed power via common ground and 12 V busbars. Verified power consumption and brightness through testing.

### 3. 3D Wiring on SolidWorks & Cable Routing
Created 3D models of wiring harnesses in SolidWorks to validate cable paths, clearances, and fixations within the chassis. Integrated high‑voltage cables (2 AWG) and low‑voltage signal wires, ensuring separation from moving parts and heat sources. This virtual validation reduced assembly errors and improved maintenance access.

### 4. Physical Installation of HV & LV Components
Led the on‑vehicle installation of all electrical components: battery packs, controller kit, DC‑DC converter, shunt resistors, throttle pedal, kill switches, and fuse box. Terminated wires with MF connectors, applied shrink wrap and secured harnesses – ensuring proper insulation and vibration resistance.
